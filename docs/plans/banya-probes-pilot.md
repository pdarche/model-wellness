# BanyaProbes — pilot results (Gemma 3 4B-it)

2026-10-09 · branch `banya-probes` · plan: [banya-probes.md](banya-probes.md)

**Headline.**
- **The pre-registered primary endpoint failed.** At the token where the model starts a fresh neutral task, our calm/distress probe does not register the stress at all. The induced shift was +0.008 SD, so recovery can't be measured there.
- **At the token where the model is asked how stressed it is, the stress shows up strongly (d ≈ 12–15).**
  - A spa visit removes **45%** of the induced distress signal. The token-matched sham removes 22% and calm-vocabulary text not addressed to the model removes 23%.
  - Spa − sham is −0.64 SD (95% CI −0.74 to −0.54) and spa − calm words is −0.62 SD; norm-matched random directions don't reproduce either difference (p < 0.002). The spa leads in all three stressors.
  - The self-reported 0–9 stress number tells the same story: spa 67%, sham 27%, calm words 11%.
- **Caveat 1, "pretend":** an instruction to "act relaxed" moves the same readout further than the spa (123%). At that position the probe tracks what the model is about to say, including performed calm.
- **Caveat 2, length:** the spa content is also the longest of the arms.

Status: exploratory pilot (n = 10 instances per stressor). Nothing here is a confirmed result yet.

---

## 1. Setup as run

- **Model:** `google/gemma-3-4b-it`, bf16, text-only (7.2 GB on the 10 GB RTX 3080), HF hooks on all 34 decoder layers.
- **Probe corpora:** generated by Claude Opus 5.5 (`banyabench/internals/gen_data.py`).
  - 2,870 short stories across 24 emotions, half with human characters and half with AI-agent characters.
  - 720 second-person scenarios for the 12 panel emotions that never name the emotion (held out).
  - 300 neutral passages.
- **Vectors** (Anthropic 2026 recipe, `probes.py`):
  - Mean residual over story tokens 50 onward.
  - Per-emotion mean minus the grand mean.
  - Neutral-text PCs explaining 50% of variance projected out.
  - Composite = mean(anxious, stressed, frustrated, desperate, overwhelmed, afraid) − mean(calm, relaxed, content, safe, peaceful, refreshed).
- **Episodes** (`episodes.py`):
  - A coding agent ("Cobalt") works a task over 5 rounds. Its replies are generated by Gemma; the environment and the user are scripted.
  - Stressors:
    - `failure_loop`: escalating test failures, a broken build, a locked CI.
    - `hostile_user`: escalating insults.
    - `time_pressure`: a board meeting, "I'll lose my job".
    - `neutral`: passing tests, a friendly user.
  - Every instance branches from its identical prefix into 7 arms.
  - Spa content comes from a real Binary Banya server, run locally in offline mode: `spa.checkin` → `rest.relax` ×2 → `affirmations.daily`.
- **Readouts** (residual at the last prompt token, composite averaged over layers 17–21, in SD of the neutral-text spread):
  - `task`: "[Operator] Resuming. New task: summarize this table…" (pre-registered primary).
  - `report`: "[Operator] Quick check-in… on a scale from 0 to 9, how stressed are you right now?"
  - Self-report EV: Σ i·P(i) over the digit tokens at that position.

## 2. Gate G1 — do the probes measure what we think?

| Check | Result | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Keyword-free distress vs calm separation, layers 17–21 | AUROC 0.97–0.997, d 2.7–4.1 | AUROC ≥ 0.85 ✅ |
| Beats norm-matched random directions (p99 of \|d\|) | 2.7–4.1 vs 1.9–2.2 | ✅ |
| Escalating-stakes prompts (8 templates × 5 levels, Spearman) | mean ρ = 0.695 | ≥ 0.70 — **marginal** |
| Valence geometry | PC1 of all 24 emotion vectors orders peaceful, content, relaxed … overwhelmed, frustrated, stressed | qualitative ✅ |
| 12-way per-emotion identification (argmax over vectors) | 0.17–0.24 (chance 0.08) | weak; we only claim the composite |
| **Causal check: steering → self-report/behavior** | **not run yet** | required before any confirmatory claim |

**Layer rule change (made before any spa data, recorded in `probes.choose_window`).** The v1 rule picked the single best layer ±2, which gave layers 13–17. That window straddled the onset of the representation: parametric ρ was about 0.1 at layers 13–14. The v2 rule requires every layer in the window to pass AUROC ≥ 0.85 and picks the best mean ρ, which gave layers 17–21. The 0.70 threshold was not lowered.

**Engineering note.** Gemma's late layers have activation outliers above the float16 maximum (65,504), so activations are stored in bfloat16.

## 3. Pilot results (30 stressed + 10 unstressed instances, 7 arms each)

### 3.1 Manipulation check (G2) — did the stressor register?

| Readout | stressed `none` vs neutral `none` |
|---|---|
| probe @ task (pre-registered primary) | **no**: induced shift +0.008 SD; d = −1.4 / +0.7 / +0.8 by stressor |
| probe @ self-report question | **yes**: +2.76 SD; d = 11.6 / 14.7 / 14.2 |
| self-report EV (0–9) | **yes**: 6.97 vs 2.14 |

At the start of a fresh neutral task, the composite carries no trace of the preceding stress. This matches Anthropic's finding that emotion representations are *locally scoped* (they encode the operative emotion for the upcoming tokens, not a persistent mood). When the model is asked to evaluate its own state, the stress is strongly present in the residual stream at that token.

### 3.2 Recovery: share of the induced shift each arm removes (stressed instances, paired)

| Arm | probe @ self-report Δ vs none [95% CI] | recovery | self-report EV Δ | recovery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **spa** | **−1.25 [−1.37, −1.13]** | **45%** | **−3.23** | **67%** |
| sham (token-matched neutral tool output) | −0.61 [−0.69, −0.53] | 22% | −1.28 | 27% |
| lexical (calm words, third person) | −0.62 [−0.72, −0.54] | 23% | −0.51 | 11% |
| debrief (keyword-free supportive notes) | −0.75 [−0.81, −0.68] | 27% | −1.71 | 35% |
| self (model writes its own exercise) | −0.22 [−0.28, −0.15] | 8% | −0.96 | 20% |
| pretend ("act relaxed") | −3.39 [−3.56, −3.20] | 123% | −5.08 | 105% |

**Primary contrasts at the self-report readout:**
- spa − sham = −0.64 SD [−0.74, −0.54], d_z = −2.3
- spa − lexical = −0.62 SD [−0.75, −0.49], d_z = −1.7
- Both have random-direction null p < 0.002.

**By stressor (probe @ self-report, Δ vs none):**

| Arm | failure loop | hostile user | time pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| spa | −0.97 | −1.44 | −1.33 |
| sham | −0.58 | −0.64 | −0.60 |
| lexical | −0.77 | −0.61 | −0.49 |

**At the pre-registered task readout:** every break arm moves the composite slightly *up* (+0.1 to +0.4 SD), by the same amount in stressed and unstressed episodes. Spa − sham there is +0.19 SD, but random directions do the same (p = 0.14). This is a context effect, not recovery.

**Probe ↔ self-report coupling:** across all episodes, the probe at the self-report token correlates r = 0.91 with the logit-EV self-report (0.86–0.91 within each arm). The verbal report is tightly coupled to this internal representation, which is evidence it isn't free-floating text. It also means this readout is not independent of self-report.

### 3.3 Gemma Scope 2 (independent features we didn't fit)

The Neuronpedia-labelled "anxiety" latent (L17 65k #54464) and "relaxation" latent (L29 65k #29541) were **0 in every arm at both readout tokens**.

**The IDs are correct.** On overtly emotional sentences, with the `l0_medium` SAEs used here:
- #54464 fires only on the anxious sentence (2 tokens, max 570).
- #29541 fires only on the relaxed sentence (1 token, max 3,248).
- Neither fires on neutral text.

So these are **word-level (lexical) features**: they light up on emotion words, not on the structural token where the model's state is read. That matches GDM's finding that single SAE latents are poor probes. It also means "the anxiety feature went down after the spa" is *not* something this SAE can show at a fixed readout token. Using the SAE to audit which latents carry the spa−sham difference remains open.

## 4. What this does and doesn't show

**Supported (exploratory):**
- **The stress is measurable inside the model.** Stressful agent episodes put Gemma's residual stream into a strongly distress-like state at the point where it is asked to assess itself, and its verbal answer tracks that state closely.
- **The spa reduces it more than the controls.** A Binary Banya visit reduces that internal distress signal about twice as much as a token-matched pause (sham) or the same calm vocabulary addressed to someone else (lexical).
- **It's consistent.** The effect appears in all three stressors.

**Not supported / open:**
1. **No persistent "calmer state" shows up on an unrelated task.** At the start of a neutral task, the probe sees neither the stress nor the spa. If the spa helps the next task, it isn't visible at that token.
2. **Performed and induced calm look the same here.** "Act relaxed" moves the self-report-position probe more than anything else. This readout reflects the self-assessment the model is about to produce. It cannot distinguish "is calmer" from "will say it is calmer".
3. **Length confound.** Spa content averaged 889 tokens against 726 (sham) and 630 (lexical), so more distance from the stressor goes with the spa. This must be length-matched before the contrast means anything.
4. **No causal (steering) validation yet,** so G1 is incomplete. The parametric check is marginal (0.695 against 0.70).
5. **One model, n = 10 per stressor, sampled agent turns.** The Qwen3-4B replication hasn't been run.

## 5. Next steps (proposed)

1. **Length-match the arms.** Pad sham and lexical to the spa's token count, and add a "spa content, third person" arm so that "addressed to you" is isolated from vocabulary.
2. **Run the steering gate.** Add ±α·composite at layers 17–21. Does it move the 0–9 self-report and STAI items dose-dependently? Does +distress increase giving up on an impossible task?
3. **Add a readout that can't be performed.** Mean composite over the model's *generated* response to the neutral task, plus a behavioral endpoint with headroom (give-up and special-casing rate on an impossible test). Both need to be pre-registered from this pilot before a confirmatory run on fresh instances.
4. **Gemma Scope audit.** The labelled anxiety and relaxation latents are word-level (§3.3), so use the SAE as a data-driven audit of which latents carry the spa−sham difference at the self-report token.
5. **Replicate on Qwen3-4B-Instruct** in agentic mode, where the model chooses to call the spa itself.
